One image.
Your Mac and your CI.

Graft bakes one golden macOS VM from a .graft recipe, then uses it as your dev box and as ephemeral GitHub Actions runners. Same image, so "works on my machine" stops being a thing.

the vocabulary, in four words

seed.graft

A declarative recipe: runtimes, toolchains, simulators.

saplinggolden image

One baked Tart VM, caches and all.

nestdev box

Shell in or open VS Code over Remote-SSH.

leafCI runner

Boots fresh, runs one job, destroyed.

arboristthe tender

Watches the tree, registers JIT runners, scales across hosts with Orchard.
